Dahyun Chung

I'm an Integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in Artificial Intelligence at KAIST AI, advised by Professor Seungryong Kim. My research lies in computer vision, with a focus on Video Generation and Vision-Language Models.

Before joining KAIST, I received my B.S. in Life Science and Data Science (double major) from Korea University, and worked as an undergraduate research intern at the KAIST Computer Vision Lab.
